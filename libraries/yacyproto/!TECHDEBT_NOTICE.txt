TECHDEBT NOTICE - yacyproto needs a refactor

Cognitive complexity is high and the package exports more than it should.

It should export the request and response structs and the functions that
encode and decode them. Nothing else.

EncodeSeed breaks that rule. A seed is not a request or a response, so
its exported encoder is a sign that either a caller uses the wire
encoding where it should not, or the business logic does not hold strict
domain types and leans on the wire form instead.

Both readings are true today. rosterEntryValueCodec in
yacynode/internal/peerroster stores a seed as its wire bytes, so the
protocol schema is also the vault schema.

Fix the exported surface first. The rest of the debt follows from it.
